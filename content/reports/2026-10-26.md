## Summary

I reported that the application for a 36-metre telecommunications tower on Stud Road near Police Road has been withdrawn, after more than 150 objections and a petition of more than 780 names, and thanked residents on both sides of Dandenong Creek for the way they went about it. I noted the work under way to recruit Council's next Chief Executive Officer, and reported from the Knox Disability Advisory Committee on the Commonwealth's Thriving Kids program and the draft public toilet management policy, which is open for comment until 1 November. I also thanked Mayor Paige Kennett at her final full meeting in the chair.

## Transcript

Thank you, Mayor. A few things from Tirhatuan Ward from the past month.

Firstly, the proposed telecommunications tower at Police Road, which was a planning application that we spoke about at the monthly meeting last month. Last week it was confirmed the applicant withdrew the application. It was for a 36-metre high phone tower in the Dandenong Creek corridor. We had more than 150 objections come in, and a petition with more than 780 names on it presented to Council. The Department of Transport and Planning had objected to that. So too had the City of Greater Dandenong, and as part of the liaison regarding that application I heard from residents on both sides of the creek down there, on the Dandenong and the Knox side. And I just wanted to acknowledge the way the residents went about that work. They were across the detail, had read the application, got their objections in to the right people at the right time, and really worked through it quite diligently. So that's how the system is meant to work, and it certainly did that time. I have conveyed to those residents that there's nothing to stop the application coming back, either in the same or a slightly different form, and we'll cross that bridge if we come to it. But the community has certainly said what it wanted to say in relation to that proposal.

Secondly, a bit of time over the last few weeks in meetings as part of the process to recruit our next Chief Executive Officer. Not much more to say about that, other than the work is being done properly and with care by all councillors, and I just want to acknowledge the Mayor's stewardship through that process to date, which is continuing.

Third, the Disability Advisory Committee met on the 6th of October. Councillor Baker and I were there, along with Councillor Considine and the Mayor. It was a joint sitting with the Early Years Advisory Committee. Just a couple of things I wanted to mention from KDAC. The committee heard from Noah's Ark. We had a briefing regarding the Thriving Kids program, which is a Commonwealth-delivered program that is going to take early childhood support for children under nine out of the NDIS and into a new state-delivered system from next year, we're told. So we had lots of quite sharp questions from committee members about how those assessments will be done, whether families will get locked into timelines, and how they continue to receive supports during that transition. We don't have answers to those questions yet, but I would just say to families in Knox who are watching this: that committee is on to it, and we'll continue asking questions as that time frame and change approaches.

The second item on the agenda which I wanted to mention was consultation on the draft public toilet management policy, which is open until the 1st of November. The advice and insight from the Disability Advisory Committee was very practical in terms of thinking about our public toilets, with suggestions around light and visibility, handwashing that can be accessed without needing to go inside, and automatic locks that fail open when the power goes out. So, lots of good insight, and I would encourage the community to engage in the consultation relating to that policy if they've got something to say.

And just very briefly, Mayor, I know this is your final full meeting of Council, and perhaps your last time listening to the ward reports, which you have probably spent many hours listening to over the past year, always with a smile and patience. But I just wanted to acknowledge, at our final full meeting of this Council year, your work in the mayoral chair. It's been a year of opportunity and challenge in equal measure, and thank you for the leadership of the group. It will continue, and we'll have more to say, I'm sure, down the track, but I just wanted to note that tonight. Thank you, Mayor.
