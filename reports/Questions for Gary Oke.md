# Questions for Gary Oke

Checklist to confirm the partnering route into the Oke bookmaking business. Grouped by what each answer settles. Prepared 9 October 2026 alongside "Victorian bookmaker licence feasibility".

## A. Structure (decides your entry route)

1. Is the business a VGCCC trading partnership of registered individuals, or a corporation with a nominee? If a corporation, who is the nominee and who are the 10%+ shareholders?
2. Who are the three partners and what VBA class is each in?
3. Does the online business hold its own Racing Victoria off-course premises approval, or does it trade under Norm's on-course licence?
4. Where is the approved off-course premises, and who is rostered there when bets are accepted? Is a Racing Victoria approved substitute used?

## B. Your admission (decides timeline)

5. Will the VBA Board treat you as joining their existing online business, so the business's class applies rather than Class I? Has the VBA ever told them how a new partner would be classified?
6. Would they register you as a key employee and seek Racing Victoria substitute approval now? What would that involve on race days?
7. What capital contribution, profit share and voting share would a fourth partner get, and what are the buy-out and succession terms if Norm retires or dies?
8. What are the current VBA Base Guarantee, guarantee-unit amounts and annual subscription they actually pay?

## C. Compliance history (decides probity exposure)

9. Is the OkeBet matter fined A$100,000 and censured by the VGCCC in September 2024, upheld at VCAT in January 2026, their business? What remediation was imposed and has it closed?
10. Any current or past VGCCC, Racing Victoria, ACMA or AUSTRAC conditions, warnings or audits on the business?

## D. The platform contract (decides your take)

11. Who is the provider, and what is the 60% calculated on: gross win, net revenue after GST and race-fields fees, or profit?
12. Who pays race-fields fees, point-of-consumption tax, GST, BetStop checks and ID verification?
13. Fixed or minimum monthly fees, contract term, exclusivity, notice period and exit fees.
14. Who owns the customer database and can it be taken to another provider?
15. Who holds the merchant facility and client funds, and are trading losses shared with the platform?
16. Has the provider said anything about the Tabcorp acquisition and continuity for small bookmakers?

## E. The real numbers (decides whether it is worth it)

17. Monthly P&L for each site for the last 12 to 24 months: turnover, hold, bonus-bet cost, overheads, net to partners.
18. Worst single month and worst single day, and how it was funded.
19. What share of turnover comes from professional, arbitrage or minimum-bet-limit accounts?
20. Hours per week each partner actually puts in, and what "minimal marketing" consists of in practice.

## F. Your own file (decides VBA class on the published criteria)

21. Confirm your race-meeting count as a clerk and which bookmakers will write the VBA's employment letters.
22. Ask whether the VBA has ever discounted a partner's clerking as too long ago.

## Answers so far (Gary, 9 October 2026 by text)

- Timeline: the Oke family will support becoming an online bookmaker "over the next 1.5 years". Consistent with the key-employee route estimate.
- Route: become a registered sole trader; the VBA guarantee is the gate and the Okes will support it; the VGCCC approves sole traders; the VBA will want to see key-employee work, so stale clerking alone is not expected to be enough. Answers questions 5 (in part) and 6.
- What is in it for them: "helping my best mate" plus "bringing in someone for fresh partnerships", i.e. a new partnership and site with a new registered partner rather than admission to the existing OkeBet partnership.

Open after this reply: questions 1 to 4 and 7 to 20, plus two new ones. First, the proposed split and capital contribution for a fresh partnership. Second, whether the VBA applies the two-year on-course-only restriction to a new online partnership whose new member enters from an on-course class.
